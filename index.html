VIVA HIGH SCHOOL

WELCOME TO OUR PAGE!

Welcome to Viva High School, a vibrant center of academic excellence beautifully situated in the serene outskirts of Wakiso District, Uganda. At Viva High, we are dedicated to nurturing well-rounded leaders by offering a comprehensive curriculum that spans both Ordinary and Advanced levels. Our rigorous academic programs are supported by top-tier facilities, including fully equipped science laboratories, a high-tech computer lab, and a resource-rich library that fuels intellectual curiosity. Beyond the classroom, our students thrive through a dynamic co-curricular program, engaging in competitive sports, vibrant Music, Dance, and Drama clubs, and an active debate society. By combining high academic standards with a supportive community, Viva High School empowers young minds to discover their talents, build lasting friendships, and prepare for global success.
